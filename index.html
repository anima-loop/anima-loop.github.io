About

Animation in motion.

ANIMA LOOP is RMIT’s annual student animation awards and showcase. It has two components: a curated exhibition of Digital Media student work and an awards competition open to every RMIT student whose work involves animation.

Why “Loop”

Animation’s oldest magic is the loop: movement that returns to its beginning. ANIMA LOOP takes its name from that endless craft.
